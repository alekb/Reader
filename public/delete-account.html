Delete your Reader Quest account

In the app (immediate):

  1. Open Reader Quest and tap Parents, then answer the grown-up question.
  2. Open the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and all data, enter your password and confirm.

This permanently deletes the parent account, the child's progress, session history and any voice recordings, from our cloud backup and from the device.

Without the app: email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We delete all data within 30 days and confirm by email.
